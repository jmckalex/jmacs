\section{Introduction}

This document exists to exercise RefTeX citations (\texttt{C-c [}).
Put point somewhere in this paragraph and press \texttt{C-c [}: choose a
cite format in the bottom menu, then pick one or more entries (press
\texttt{m} to mark several) from \texttt{cite-demo.bib}. For example,
a single cite goes here ---\ and a multi-key cite goes here ---.
